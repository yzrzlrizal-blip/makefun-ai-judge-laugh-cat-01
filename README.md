One funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human, under 200 characters. The cat's complaint: breakfast is served at 6am sharp, yet the human acts surprised when the cat starts screaming at 5:59 — the schedule was in the contract, and the cat ate the contract.

> One star. He fills my bowl at 6am, then acts surprised when I scream at 5:59. The schedule was in the contract. I ate the contract.

`main.py` checks the review in `entry.txt` against the gate (one line, 1–200 characters, no control characters) and publishes it as `entry.txt` in the output folder the grader provides through `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
